How do I send a fax?

  1. Open the Send Fax tab and enter the 10-digit fax number, including the area code.
  2. Tap Scan to photograph paper pages, or add Photos or PDF Files.
  3. Optionally turn on the cover sheet and add a subject and message.
  4. Tap Send Fax and confirm. The fax appears in History with its status.

What do the statuses mean?

  • Queued: your fax was received and is waiting to be dialed.
  • Sending: the receiving machine answered and pages are being transmitted. This usually takes about a minute per page.
  • Delivered: the receiving machine confirmed every page.
  • Failed: the fax couldn't be delivered, for example because the line was busy, no fax machine answered or the number was wrong. The reason is shown in the app, and failed pages don't count toward your weekly allowance. Tap Send Again to retry.

Which numbers can I fax?

Fax numbers in the United States and Canada. International numbers and premium-rate numbers aren't supported yet.

How much can I send?

Fax & Scan Pro includes up to 100 pages in any rolling 7 days, and up to 50 pages per fax. Settings shows how many pages you've used this week.

Is the cover sheet maker free?

Yes. Open the Cover Sheet tab, fill it in and tap Preview to print it or share it as a PDF. No subscription is needed.

Subscriptions

  • Restore a purchase: Settings → Restore Purchases, or tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen.
  • Cancel: Settings → Manage Subscription, or the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal (or before the free trial ends) to avoid being charged.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I receive faxes?

Not yet. Fax and Scan sends faxes only.
