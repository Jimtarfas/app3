Fax and Scan: Send Fax Online

Send a fax from your iPhone in under a minute. No fax machine, no phone line.

  • Scan documents with the camera. Edges are found and pages are flattened automatically.
  • Add photos and PDF files from your library or the Files app.
  • Free fax cover sheet maker. Fill in the sender, recipient and message, then print it, share it as a PDF or send it with your fax.
  • Send to any fax number in the United States and Canada.
  • Delivery confirmation and a history of everything you've sent.

Sending faxes needs a Fax & Scan Pro subscription (weekly, or yearly with a 3-day free trial) and includes up to 100 pages every 7 days. Scanning and the cover sheet maker are free.
