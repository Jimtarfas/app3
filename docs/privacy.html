Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Fax and Scan: Send Fax Online ("Fax and Scan", "the app", "we") lets you scan documents and send them as faxes. To send a fax, the document has to leave your iPhone. This policy explains exactly what is sent, who receives it and how long it is kept.

In short: there are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Scans, cover sheets and your fax history stay on your iPhone. When you send a fax, the document goes over an encrypted connection to our fax server and on to our fax carrier, and only for as long as it takes to deliver it. We keep a small delivery record for 30 days.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Documents you scan or import, before you send them.
  • Copies of the faxes you have sent, and your fax history.
  • The sender details you enter in Settings (name, company, phone, fax, email), used to fill in cover sheets.
  • Cover sheets you make with the free cover sheet maker.

We never receive this information unless it is part of a document you choose to fax. Deleting a fax from History, or deleting the app, removes it from your device.

What is sent when you fax a document

When you tap Send Fax, the app uploads the following over HTTPS to our fax server:

  • The document (a PDF of your pages, including the cover sheet if you added one).
  • The destination fax number.
  • A random identifier created by the app when it is installed. It is not linked to your name, Apple Account or device hardware, and is used only so the app can look up the delivery status of your faxes.
  • The signed App Store transaction for your subscription, so the server can confirm the subscription is active. It contains the product, purchase and expiry dates and a transaction ID. It does not contain your name, email or payment details.

Our server passes the document and destination number straight to our fax carrier, Telnyx, which dials the number and transmits the fax. Our server does not save your document; it is held in memory only while it is forwarded. The carrier processes it to deliver the fax and handles it under its own privacy policy and data protection terms.

We do not sell or share this information, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.

Payments

Subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Camera and photos

  • Camera: used only when you scan a document. Scans are not saved to your Photos library.
  • Photos and Files: the app only gets the specific photos or files you pick. It does not ask for access to your whole photo library.

Security

All communication between the app, our server and the fax carrier is encrypted with TLS. Keep in mind that the fax network itself is not encrypted once a fax is transmitted to the receiving machine, so avoid faxing information you would not send through any other fax service.

Your choices and requests

You can delete any fax from your History at any time. Server records are deleted automatically after 30 days. Because we don't know who you are, we can't look you up by name; if you want your records deleted sooner, email us and we'll explain how to identify them from the app.

Children

Fax and Scan is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
