# Beta testers for Fax and Scan

TestFlight link: https://testflight.apple.com/join/nssfEV2b (works once Apple approves the beta, limited to 100 testers)

## Who to ask

The best testers actually have to fax something: people dealing with clinics, pharmacies, insurers, landlords, title companies or government offices. Places to ask:

- Friends, family and colleagues (ideally 5–10 people who really fax)
- Reddit: r/TestFlight, r/iosbeta, r/apphookup (read each subreddit's rules first)
- BetaList or similar beta directories
- Local small-business or real-estate groups

## Short post (copy, edit and share)

> I'm testing a new iPhone app, **Fax and Scan**. You scan a document with your camera, add a cover sheet and send it as a fax to any US or Canada fax number, without a fax machine.
>
> It's free while in TestFlight, including up to 20 fax pages a week. If you ever have to fax a form to a doctor, pharmacy, insurer or office, I'd love for you to try it and tell me what's confusing or missing.
>
> Join here: https://testflight.apple.com/join/nssfEV2b
> To send feedback, take a screenshot in the app and tap "Share Beta Feedback".

## What to collect for the App Review reply

For each useful piece of feedback, note:

- What the tester tried, what went wrong or was missing, and roughly when.
- What we changed in response, and in which build.

Question 4 of Apple's reply asks for real examples of beta feedback applied to the production build. Use only feedback that actually came in.
